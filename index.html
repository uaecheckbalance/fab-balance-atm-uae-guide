Checking Your FAB Balance at an ATM in the UAE: A Simple Walkthrough

By Daniel Collins

ATMs are still one of the quickest ways to confirm a First Abu Dhabi Bank balance, especially if your phone battery is low or you are already out. The process is short, it needs no app, and it gives you a printed or on screen result in under a minute. Here is how it works, plus what to do when the figure on screen does not match what you expected.

Start by using a FAB ATM, or any ATM that accepts your card, and insert your card. Enter your PIN carefully, shielding the keypad with your other hand. From the main menu, choose the balance enquiry option, then pick the account you want to check, usually your current or salary account. The machine shows your available balance on screen, and most machines offer a printed mini statement as well. Take your card and any receipt before you walk away.

There are a few things worth knowing. The balance shown is your available balance, which can differ from a pending salary credit that has not fully cleared yet. If you recently received a payment, wait a short while and check again before assuming something is wrong. Avoid ATMs that look damaged or have loose parts around the card slot, and never accept help from a stranger at the machine.

If you do not want to visit an ATM at all, the other methods are just as simple. You can check your balance in the FAB Mobile app once you are registered, or send BAL plus the last four digits of your card to 2121 by SMS. For a fuller comparison of every method, this complete FAB balance check walkthrough covers the app, SMS, ATM and online routes in one place.

When should you call the bank? If your balance drops after a transaction you do not recognise, if a salary credit is missing for more than a day, or if the ATM keeps your card, call FAB customer care on 600 52 5500 straight away. Ratibi card holders can also use the Ratibi helpline on 600 52 2298. Keep the receipt or note the ATM location and time, because the bank will ask for those details.
